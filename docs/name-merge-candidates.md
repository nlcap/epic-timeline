# Creator-name merge candidates — full sweep across all five collections

Run after the last collection landed. **2,056 distinct names over 13,205 credit
slots.** Five passes (accents/punctuation, Jr./Sr. suffix, middle initial,
nickname vs formal, edit distance ≤ 2), then each candidate pair tested against
the Marvel and DC wikis, where a redirect from one spelling to the other is the
databases asserting they are one person.

Counts below are credit slots; the collections a name appears in are in brackets.
Nothing here has been applied.

## E. Two traps in the method itself

**A redirect can land on a character, not a creator.** `Matt Ryan` redirects to
**Red Ryan** and `Jim Sanders` to **Speed Demon** — both characters. An automated
pass trusting redirects would rename an inker into a supervillain. Any merge
must confirm the target is a creator page.

**Studio credits look like people and must be left alone:** `Crusty Bunkers`,
`The Tribe`, `Shuster Shop`, `Deodato Studios`, `Comicraft`.

## F. O'Neil and Owsley — done

Applied across every collection, matching the DC Finest convention:

- **`Dennis "Denny" O'Neil`** now reads the same way in all **32** of his credit
  slots — 21 in DC Finest, 11 recovered in Classic Marvel, which the original
  pass never reached.
- **`Jim Owsley (Christopher Priest)`** on all **8** Owsley-era credits (7 in
  Classic Marvel, 1 in DC Finest) — the name each book was published under, with
  the name he goes by now.
- **`Christopher Priest`** stays plain on the **4** credits published that way.

`Kevin O'Neill` is a different person with a different surname and was matched
exactly, never by substring, so he is untouched.

## G. Held back deliberately — not oversights

Creators who changed their name are recorded as published: **Jim Owsley**
(annotated "(Christopher Priest)"), **Tony Isabella**, **Andrew Pepoy**, **Dennis
Cramer**, **Barbara Randall**, **Joyce Murchison**. The wikis redirect all of
these to later names; the published credit stands unless you decide otherwise.
